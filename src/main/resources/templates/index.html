About Super ERP

Built by developers, for businesses. Super ERP is a comprehensive enterprise management platform designed to streamline operations across all departments.

Our mission is to democratize enterprise software by providing a production-ready, feature-rich ERP system that traditionally costs lakhs of rupees. With Super ERP, businesses of all sizes can access enterprise-grade tools without the enterprise price tag.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Super ERP

The Starter plan is free forever and supports up to 2 users with limited billing (100 invoices/month). The Professional plan is ₹4,999/month, supports unlimited users and all 14+ modules including HR, CRM, Project Management, and full GST compliance with priority support.

For most businesses, the initial setup takes less than a day. Our team configures your GST settings, product categories, user roles, and basic workflows during onboarding. Professional and Enterprise plan customers get dedicated onboarding support.

Absolutely. The Starter plan is free and perfect for small businesses. As you grow, you can upgrade to Professional. Our pricing is flat per company — not per user — so there's no penalty for adding team members.

Starter plans get email support (48-hour response). Professional plans get priority email and phone support (8-hour response). Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone support, a dedicated account manager, and quarterly business reviews.

Yes, we support migration from most popular ERPs including Tally, Zoho Books, and custom systems. Our team provides data migration assistance on Professional and Enterprise plans. We'll help map your existing data structure, perform test migrations, and ensure zero data loss.

Super ERP is fully GST-compliant. We support GSTR-1, GSTR-3B reports, e-invoicing via NIC portal, and e-waybill generation. Our system automatically calculates CGST, SGST, IGST based on your configuration. HSN/SAC codes are pre-loaded and we update GST rates as per government notifications.

Yes! Our Progressive Web App (PWA) works on both Android and iOS devices. You can access all features including expense claims, attendance, job cards, and approvals from your mobile. The app works offline and syncs when you're back online.

You own your data, always. Before cancellation, you can export all your data in standard formats (CSV, Excel, JSON). We provide a 30-day grace period after cancellation where you can still access and download your data. After that, data is securely deleted as per our data retention policy.
